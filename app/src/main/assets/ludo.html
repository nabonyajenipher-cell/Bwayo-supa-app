CLASSIC LUDO RULES
• Each player has four counters. Roll a 6 to enter a counter from the yard.
• Move clockwise around the track, then up your own coloured home lane.
• You must roll the exact number to reach the centre finish.
• Capture opponents on ordinary squares; star squares are safe.
• Rolling a 6 gives another roll. Three consecutive sixes cancel the turn. First player to bring all four counters home wins.

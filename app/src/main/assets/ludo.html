Roll the dice. You need a 6 to bring a counter out of your yard.
CLASSIC LUDO RULES
• Each player has four counters. Roll a 6 to enter a counter from the yard.
• Move clockwise around the 52-square track, then up your own coloured home column.
• You must roll the exact number to reach the centre finish.
• Landing on an opponent on an ordinary square captures it and sends it back to its yard.
• Star squares are safe: counters there cannot be captured. Your own two counters on one square make a blockade that opponents cannot land on or pass.
• Rolling a 6 gives another roll. Three sixes in a row cancel the turn (common classic rule). First player to bring all four counters home wins.

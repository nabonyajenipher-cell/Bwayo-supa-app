🎲 BWAYO LUDO

OFFLINE LOCAL MULTIPLAYER • 2–4 PLAYERS • SAVES ON THIS PHONE
RED PLAYER'S TURN
Tap ROLL DICE to begin. Get a six to bring a token out.
Dice: — · Tap one of your highlighted tokens after rolling.
HOW TO PLAY
• Get a 6 to move a token out of base.
• Move around the board and enter your coloured home lane.
• You must roll the exact number to finish a token.
• Land on an opponent to send them back to base. Safe squares protect tokens.
• First player to finish all 4 tokens wins. Pass the phone between players.
